Geometric Sentinel Auditing: Timely Rehearsal in Adapter Continual Learning

Lightweight forward-only activation telemetry and manifold entanglement tripwires for event-driven continual LoRA adaptation

CodeDemoPaper
Question

Fine-tuning an adapter across a continuous stream of tasks introduces a dilemma: updating exclusively on new data overwrites low-rank directions essential to prior capabilities, while continuous uniform rehearsal burns compute and dilutes incoming gradients. Detecting impending degradation via backward passes (EWC, A-GEM) doubles latency, while autoregressive loss forecasting scales with completion length and lags behind the damage. Can we detect impending forgetting in a single non-autoregressive forward pass by monitoring internal activation geometry?

Approach

We audit a small, frozen pool of anchor inputs—sentinel prompts (K=32)—drawn from the original task distribution and strictly quarantined from training. At chunk boundaries, we evaluate these prompts in a single forward pass without autoregressive decoding or backpropagation, extracting the hidden state at the final prompt token (layer 18). Activations are decomposed via Dual Gram SVD into an in-subspace projection and a scale-free Relative Manifold Entanglement Index (MEI_rel), governed by a four-state deterministic hysteresis controller.

Methodology

Non-autoregressive forward auditing protocol, Dual Gram SVD decomposition, and four-state hysteresis actuation synthesized directly from the research repository:

  • Forward-Only Sentinel Auditing and Layer Depth Selection · Qwen2.5-0.5B & LoRA. We evaluate continual adaptation on Qwen2.5-0.5B targeting 9.44 million parameters across all attention and feed-forward projections. Rather than evaluating full completions, we capture representations at intermediate layer 18 where causal self-attention forms an information bottleneck directly preceding the classification head.
  • Subspace Decomposition and the Scalar Drift Paradox · Dual Gram SVD. We analyze why naive scalar cosine distance fails in continual learning streams. Because benign syntactic updates inhabit a task-invariant null space that absorbs 88% of representational displacement without damaging accuracy, we decompose activations via Dual Gram SVD to measure true in-subspace projection and Grassmannian canonical angle alignment.
  • Scale-Free Relative Manifold Entanglement Index · Geometric Telemetry. We compute the Relative Manifold Entanglement Index by taking the ratio between intra-class cluster dispersion and inter-class nearest-neighbor margins, normalized by subspace preservation. Under rigid coordinate rotations the index remains quiescent, but it spikes reliably whenever destructive gradient interference begins compressing class decision boundaries.
  • Closed-Loop Hysteresis Control under Fixed Prompt-Slot Budgets · 4-State Actuator. We govern experience replay through a deterministic four-state controller that couples dual coordinate and velocity shock triggers with a bounded rehearsal window and mandatory cooldown period. To ensure fair compute accounting, the total optimizer step count and token exposures remain strictly identical across all tested strategies.

Findings

Pre-emptive warning windows, scalar drift paradox resolution, and Pareto-advantageous closed-loop retention verified across sequential continual learning streams:

  • Prospective Advance Lead Time Window (τ = 1.65 at Step 20 vs Cliff at Step 30). Internal representations begin shearing well before behavioral failure occurs on surface tokens. Sentinel manifold drift crosses the calibrated tripwire at step 20, providing a 10-step operational warning window before held-out classification accuracy breaches the relative loss cliff at step 30.
  • Pareto-Dominant Retention and Plasticity Balance (74.8% Ret · 79.6% Acq · 146k GFLOPs). Closed-loop manifold auditing establishes a superior compute-retention Pareto frontier. It achieves 74.8% retention on earlier skills while preserving 79.6% plasticity on incoming tasks, strictly outperforming periodic rehearsal schedules and autoregressive loss forecasting at a lower compute budget of 146k GFLOPs.
  • Causal Necessity of Prompt Intervention Timing (-4.8 pp Loss on 3-Chunk Delay). Controlled delay sweeps prove that timely actuation is causally decisive for model preservation. Delaying the rehearsal window by only 60 steps post-detection causes immediate post-replay recovery to drop from 72.2% down to 67.4%, demonstrating that representations that drift into alternate loss basins cannot be recovered with identical replay volume.
  • Rehearsal Compute and Replay Token Conservation (17.3k vs 32.6k Replay Tokens). By triggering replay only when representational stability is genuinely compromised, the event-driven controller requires just four surgical rehearsal windows across thirty chunks. This conserves 47% of replay tokens relative to standard uniform mixing while eliminating persistent gradient dilution.
